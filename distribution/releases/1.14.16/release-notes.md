# Apogee Forever 1.14.16

For World of Warcraft Forever 1.60.1.

- See remaining shield protection directly on friendly health bars with a pale-blue striped overlay. Shields stay visible at full health and shrink as damage is absorbed.
- Track your own healing-over-time effects with white bars: Priest Renew, Druid Rejuvenation, Regrowth and Wild Growth, and Shaman Riptide. Druid effects have separate bars.
- Debuffs use fixed slots for Magic, Disease, Poison, Curse and other effects, showing the newest effect of each type. Hover for the native tooltip; highlighted borders identify types you can cleanse, and left-click casts your learned removal spell.
- Buff and debuff countdowns use larger centered white numbers without a seconds suffix during the final minute. Expiring buff reminders now appear during the last minute instead of the last two minutes. Missing buffs still appear immediately.
- Recognize the reviewed current client build without stale startup warnings.
- Fix missing debuff timers and cleanse availability after preview, combat entry and zoning.

Reviewed against Forever build 70862. The owner confirmed the matching DEV build works in game. Automated source and package checks pass; comprehensive native combat and taint acceptance remains incomplete.
