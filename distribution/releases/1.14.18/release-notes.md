# Apogee Forever 1.14.18

- Added resurrection buttons to the left of dead party members and friendly player targets, including released party members.
- Supports Priest Resurrection, Paladin Redemption, Shaman Ancestral Spirit and Druid Revive, using your highest learned rank.
- Added a separate Druid Rebirth button that remains available during combat, including when a party member dies mid-fight.
- Resurrection icons turn grey when the spell reports the recipient out of range and return to color in range. The game still checks corpse reachability, line of sight, cooldowns and reagents when you click.
- Fixed excess Rebirth range polling during frequent combat updates.

For World of Warcraft Forever 1.60.1. Regular resurrection and range feedback have been tested in game; Druid combat behavior has automated coverage and still needs live testing.
