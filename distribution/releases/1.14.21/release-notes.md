# Apogee Forever 1.14.21

- Improve stability when clicking and comparing bag items, with safer tooltip cleanup and fewer redundant item-data refreshes.
- Remove the Gear selector. Bags and the character window now show upgrades for all supported roles and playstyles, including in dungeons.
- Keep Auction House role filters and dungeon loot role guidance separate. The session loot-role fallback is available in Apogee > Stats settings.
- Contain tooltip, auction and settings errors so a failed feature does not keep generating errors.

For World of Warcraft Forever 1.60.1. Reload the UI after updating.
